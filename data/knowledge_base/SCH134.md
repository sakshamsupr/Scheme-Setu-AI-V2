# Ambedkar Hastshilp Vikas Yojana

Scheme for setting up craft-village clusters with infrastructure, design, and credit support for SC/ST artisan communities.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC;ST;Artisans
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹25000–₹300000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: 5-7%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;Caste Certificate;Artisan ID
