# West Bengal Karma Sathi Prakalpa

West Bengal self-employment scheme offering subsidized loans to youth and women to start micro-enterprises in the state.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Women;State-Specific
- Age: 18–45
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: West Bengal

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Business Plan
