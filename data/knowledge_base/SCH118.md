# National Safai Karamcharis Finance and Development Corporation (NSKFDC) Loan

Concessional credit scheme for safai karamcharis and their dependents to set up self-employment ventures.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Services;Trading;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹1500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 4-6%
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Caste Certificate;Occupation Proof
