# Udyogini Scheme

State-backed subsidized loan scheme by KSFC/state boards for women below poverty line to start small businesses.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Trading;Services;Farming
- State: Karnataka

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹300000
- Subsidy: 30%
- Interest: 0-8% (subsidized)
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Income Certificate;BPL Card
