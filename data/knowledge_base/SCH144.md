# Startup India Seed Fund Scheme

Seed funding support for early-stage startups for proof of concept, prototype development, and market entry.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;Women
- Age: 18–45
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹2000000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: NA (Grant/Equity)
- Tenure: 0 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Company Registration;Pitch Deck

## Source
https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in
