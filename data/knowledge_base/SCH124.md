# Cent Kalyani Scheme

Collateral-free credit scheme by Central Bank of India exclusively for women entrepreneurs across sectors.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹10000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per Central Bank norms
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement
