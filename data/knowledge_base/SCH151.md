# Kerala Kudumbashree Micro Enterprise Scheme

Kerala's flagship women's collective scheme offering subsidized credit and enterprise support to Self Help Group members.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women;State-Specific;Rural
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Handicrafts;Farming;Trading;Services
- State: Kerala

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 4-7%
- Tenure: 5 years

Aadhaar;Kudumbashree Membership Certificate
