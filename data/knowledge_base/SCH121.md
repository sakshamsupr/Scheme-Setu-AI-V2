# Annapurna Scheme for Women

Collateral-free loan scheme for women to set up food catering and small eatery businesses, repayable through EMIs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Trading;Services
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹50000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 10-12%
- Tenure: 3 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Guarantor Details
