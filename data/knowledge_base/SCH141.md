# PM SVANidhi

Micro-credit scheme for street vendors offering working capital loans in increasing tranches with interest subsidy on timely repayment.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Women;Rural
- Age: 18–65
- Business: Trading;Services
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹50000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 7-9% (interest subsidy on repayment)
- Tenure: 1 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;Vendor Certificate;Bank Statement
