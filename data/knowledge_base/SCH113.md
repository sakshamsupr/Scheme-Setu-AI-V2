# Venture Capital Fund for SC (IFCI)

Equity/venture capital support managed by IFCI for viable SC entrepreneur-led projects to promote entrepreneurship among Scheduled Castes.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹1000000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: Equity-based (no interest)
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Caste Certificate;Detailed Project Report
