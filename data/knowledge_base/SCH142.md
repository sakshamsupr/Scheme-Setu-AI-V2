# Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - NRLM

National Rural Livelihood Mission promoting Self Help Groups with bank-linked credit for rural women's micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women;Rural
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Farming;Handicrafts;Trading;Services
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 0-7% (SHG linked)
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;SHG Membership Certificate;Bank Statement
