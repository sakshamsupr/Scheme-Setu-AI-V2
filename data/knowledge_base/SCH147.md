# Gujarat Garvi Gurjari Artisan Support Scheme

Gujarat state scheme offering marketing platforms, subsidy, and credit linkage for traditional artisans and craftspeople.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: Artisans;State-Specific
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: Gujarat

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Artisan ID
