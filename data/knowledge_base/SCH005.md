# PM Mudra Kishor

Collateral-free loans between 50,000 and 5 lakh rupees for growing micro and small businesses under the Mudra Yojana.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–65
- Business: Trading;Services;Manufacturing;Farming
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50001–₹500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 9-13%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement
