# Adivasi Mahila Sashaktikaran Yojana

State-run empowerment scheme offering subsidized credit and training to tribal women for micro-enterprise development.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: ST;Women
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Handicrafts;Farming;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹25000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 4-7%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;ST Certificate;Business Plan
