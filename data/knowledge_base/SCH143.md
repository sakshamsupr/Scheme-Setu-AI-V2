# Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - NULM

National Urban Livelihood Mission offering subsidized loans and skill training for urban poor self-employment and micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Women;Urban
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Trading;Services;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 5-7% (subsidized)
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Income Certificate;Bank Statement
