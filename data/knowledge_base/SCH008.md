# National SC-ST Hub (NSSH)

Facilitation of bank loans, capacity building, marketing assistance, and handholding support for SC/ST entrepreneurs and enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC;ST
- Age: 18–60
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹1000000–₹10000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per lending bank
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Caste Certificate;Business Plan
