# Mahila Coir Yojana

Subsidy-linked self-employment scheme for rural women in coir-producing states to set up motorized ratt units.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women;Rural
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹300000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 6-8% (subsidized)
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Coir Board Registration
