# Odisha Mission Shakti

Odisha state mission empowering women Self Help Groups through interest-free credit and livelihood support programs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women;State-Specific;Rural
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Farming;Handicrafts;Trading;Services
- State: Odisha

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 0-7% (SHG linked)
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;SHG Membership Certificate;Domicile Certificate
