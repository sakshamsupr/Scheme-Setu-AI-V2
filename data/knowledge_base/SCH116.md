# Dr. Ambedkar Scheme for Social Integration through Inter-Caste Marriages

One-time financial incentive scheme supporting families for social integration, indirectly aiding household enterprise stability.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC;ST
- Age: 21–60
- Business: Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹250000–₹250000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: NA (Grant)
- Tenure: 0 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;Caste Certificate;Marriage Certificate

https://socialjustice.gov.in
