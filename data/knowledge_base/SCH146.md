# Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme - Special Component

Enhanced-subsidy component of PMEGP targeted specifically at SC/ST, women, and special category applicants.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC;ST;Women;Rural
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Handicrafts;Farming
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 35%
- Interest: 4-8% (subsidized)
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Caste Certificate;Project Report
