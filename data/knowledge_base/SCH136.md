# Silk Samagra Scheme

Central Silk Board scheme offering financial and technical assistance for sericulture and silk-based micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Rural;Artisans
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Farming;Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: 6-8%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Sericulture Registration
