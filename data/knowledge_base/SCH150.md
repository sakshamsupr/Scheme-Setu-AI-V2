# Bihar Mukhyamantri Udyami Yojana

Bihar state scheme providing interest-free loans with 50% grant component to first-generation entrepreneurs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;Women;OBC
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: Bihar

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 50%
- Interest: 0% (interest-free)
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Educational Certificate
