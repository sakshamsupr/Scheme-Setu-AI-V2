# PM Vishwakarma

Support scheme for traditional artisans and craftspeople offering skill training, toolkit incentive, and collateral-free credit in two tranches.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;OBC;SC;ST
- Age: 18–60
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹300000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 5%
- Tenure: 3 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Artisan ID Card;Skill Certificate
