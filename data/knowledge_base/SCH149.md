# Madhya Pradesh Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana

MP state self-employment scheme offering margin money subsidy and bank loan linkage for new micro-enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–45
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: Madhya Pradesh

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 30%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Business Plan
