# Karnataka Vishwa/Chief Minister's SC/ST Entrepreneur Scheme

Karnataka state capital subsidy and interest subsidy scheme for SC/ST entrepreneurs establishing new micro and small enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC;ST;State-Specific
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: Karnataka

## Finance
- Loan: ₹500000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 50%
- Interest: 0-4% (subsidized)
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;Caste Certificate;Domicile Certificate;Business Plan
