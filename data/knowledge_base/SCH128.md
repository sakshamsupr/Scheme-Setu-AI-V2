# Rajasthan Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavlamban Yojana

Rajasthan state scheme offering collateral-free loans and interest subvention to young entrepreneurs and startups.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–45
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: Rajasthan

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹2500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Business Plan
