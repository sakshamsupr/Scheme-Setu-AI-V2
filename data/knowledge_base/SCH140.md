# Minorities Micro Finance Scheme (NMDFC)

National Minorities Development & Finance Corporation scheme for small credit to minority entrepreneurs, with priority to women.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Women
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Trading;Services;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹25000–₹200000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 6-8%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;Minority Certificate;Income Certificate
