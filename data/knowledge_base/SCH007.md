# Lakhpati Didi

Skilling and interest-free/low-interest credit support to Self Help Group women members to help households earn at least 1 lakh rupees annually.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women;Rural
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Farming;Handicrafts;Trading;Services
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 0-7% (SHG linked)
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;SHG Membership Certificate;Bank Statement
