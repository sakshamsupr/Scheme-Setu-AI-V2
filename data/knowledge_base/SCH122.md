# Bhartiya Mahila Bank Business Loan (merged with SBI)

Concessional interest-rate business loan scheme for women entrepreneurs, now operated through SBI branches nationwide.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹2000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per SBI norms
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement
