# Handloom Weavers Comprehensive Welfare Scheme

Central scheme offering credit access, insurance, and margin money support to traditional handloom weavers and artisan clusters.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: Artisans;General
- Age: 18–60
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹25000–₹300000
- Subsidy: 20%
- Interest: 6-7%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Weaver ID Card;Bank Statement
