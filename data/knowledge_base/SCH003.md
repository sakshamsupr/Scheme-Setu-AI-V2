# PMEGP Urban

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme for setting up new micro-enterprises in urban areas with margin money subsidy.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: 4-8% (subsidized)
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Project Report;Address Proof
