# Punjab Ghar Ghar Rozgar Scheme

Punjab state scheme offering subsidized loans and employment-linked support for youth and marginalized entrepreneurs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;Women;Rural
- Age: 18–45
- Business: Manufacturing;Trading;Services
- State: Punjab

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹2500000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Business Plan
