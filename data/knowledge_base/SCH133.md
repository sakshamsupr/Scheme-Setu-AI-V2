# National Handicraft Development Programme (NHDP)

Central scheme providing design development, marketing support, and credit facilitation for traditional handicraft artisans and clusters.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: Artisans;General
- Age: 18–60
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 20%
- Interest: 6-8%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Artisan Card;Business Plan
