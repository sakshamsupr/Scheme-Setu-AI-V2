# Uttar Pradesh One District One Product (ODOP) Scheme

UP state scheme promoting district-specific traditional products with subsidized loans, training and marketing support for artisans.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Artisans;State-Specific
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: Uttar Pradesh

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹2500000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Artisan ID
