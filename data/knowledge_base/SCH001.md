# Stand-Up India

Bank loans between 10 lakh and 1 crore for at least one SC/ST and one woman borrower per bank branch to set up a greenfield enterprise.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC;ST;Women
- Age: 18–65
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹1000000–₹10000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: MCLR + 3% + tenor premium
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Caste Certificate;Business Plan;Bank Statement
