# Rural Self Employment Training Institutes (RSETI) Support

Free skill training and post-training credit linkage support for rural youth and micro-entrepreneurs to start self-employment ventures.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women;Rural
- Age: 18–45
- Business: Farming;Handicrafts;Services;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per bank linkage
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;RSETI Training Certificate;Bank Statement
