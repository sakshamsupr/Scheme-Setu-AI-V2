# CGTMSE

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises providing collateral-free credit guarantee cover to lending institutions.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–65
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹0–₹20000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per lending bank + guarantee fee
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement;Udyam Registration
