# Maharashtra Udyogini Yojana

Maharashtra state scheme offering interest subsidy on loans for women entrepreneurs setting up new enterprises.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women;State-Specific
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Trading;Services
- State: Maharashtra

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹2500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Business Plan
