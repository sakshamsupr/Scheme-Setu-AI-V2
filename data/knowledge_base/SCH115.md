# Credit Enhancement Guarantee Scheme for SCs

Guarantee cover facilitating bond/loan raising by SC entrepreneurs for green-field ventures at competitive rates.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: SC
- Age: 21–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹10000000–₹50000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: Market linked (guarantee cover)
- Tenure: 10 years

## Documents
PAN;Caste Certificate;Company Registration;Bond Documents
