# TREAD Scheme for Women

Trade Related Entrepreneurship Assistance and Development scheme offering credit and training support via NGOs for women entrepreneurs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹3000000
- Subsidy: 30%
- Interest: As per lending institution
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;NGO Recommendation;Business Plan
