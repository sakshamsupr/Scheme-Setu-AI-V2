# PM Mudra Shishu

Collateral-free micro-loans up to 50,000 rupees for very small and starting-stage businesses under the Mudra Yojana.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–65
- Business: Trading;Services;Manufacturing;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹0–₹50000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 8-12%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement

https://www.mudra.org.in
