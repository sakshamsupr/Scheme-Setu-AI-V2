# Design & Technology Upgradation Scheme

Central scheme supporting artisans in adopting improved tools, design innovation, and modern production techniques.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: Artisans;General
- Age: 18–60
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 20%
- Interest: 6-8%
- Tenure: 5 years

Aadhaar;PAN;Artisan Card
