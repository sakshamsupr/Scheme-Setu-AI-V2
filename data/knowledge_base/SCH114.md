# Venture Capital Fund for ST

Equity/venture capital support scheme for Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs to promote self-employment and enterprise creation.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: ST
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹1000000–₹5000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: Equity-based (no interest)
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Caste Certificate;Detailed Project Report
