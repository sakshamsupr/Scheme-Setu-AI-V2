# PM Mudra Tarun

Collateral-free loans between 5 lakh and 10 lakh rupees for established micro-enterprises seeking expansion under the Mudra Yojana.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;OBC;Women
- Age: 18–65
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹500001–₹1000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 10-14%
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement;ITR
