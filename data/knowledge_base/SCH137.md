# Coir Vikas Yojana

Coir Board scheme providing subsidy-linked loans and skill training to coir-based micro-enterprises and artisans.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Rural;Artisans
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Handicrafts;Manufacturing
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹500000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: 6-8%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Coir Board Registration
