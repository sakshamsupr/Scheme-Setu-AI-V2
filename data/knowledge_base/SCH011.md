# Mahila Udyam Nidhi Scheme

Financial assistance scheme by SIDBI/PNB for women entrepreneurs to set up new small-scale ventures or expand existing ones.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: Women
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Services;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹100000–₹2500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per SIDBI/PNB norms
- Tenure: 10 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Bank Statement
