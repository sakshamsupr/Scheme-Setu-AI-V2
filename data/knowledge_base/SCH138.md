# National Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation (NBCFDC) Term Loan

Concessional term loan scheme for OBC entrepreneurs to set up income-generating self-employment ventures.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: OBC
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Trading;Services;Farming
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹1500000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 4-6%
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;OBC Certificate;Income Certificate

## Source
https://nbcfdc.gov.in
