# Tamil Nadu New Entrepreneur-cum-Enterprise Development Scheme (NEEDS)

Tamil Nadu government scheme providing capital subsidy and interest subvention for first-generation entrepreneurs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;SC;ST;Women
- Age: 21–50
- Business: Manufacturing;Services
- State: Tamil Nadu

## Finance
- Loan: ₹1000000–₹10000000
- Subsidy: 25%
- Interest: Interest subvention
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Domicile Certificate;Educational Certificate;Business Plan
