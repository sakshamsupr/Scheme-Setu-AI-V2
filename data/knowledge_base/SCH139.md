# NBCFDC Mahila Samriddhi Yojana

Micro-finance scheme under NBCFDC offering small collateral-free loans to OBC women for micro-enterprise activities.

## Eligibility
- Gender: Women
- Category: OBC;Women
- Age: 18–50
- Business: Trading;Services;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹25000–₹150000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 4-5%
- Tenure: 5 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;OBC Certificate;Income Certificate

## Source
https://nbcfdc.gov.in
