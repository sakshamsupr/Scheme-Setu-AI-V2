# National Scheduled Tribes Finance and Development Corporation (NSTFDC) Term Loan

Concessional term loans for income-generating activities and micro-enterprises exclusively for Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: ST
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Farming;Handicrafts;Trading
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹50000–₹10000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: 6-8%
- Tenure: 8 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;ST Certificate;Project Report
