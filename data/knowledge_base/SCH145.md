# Aspire Scheme

Scheme promoting innovation, rural entrepreneurship, and agro-industry through Livelihood Business Incubators and technology centres.

## Eligibility
- Gender: All
- Category: General;Rural;SC;ST
- Age: 18–55
- Business: Manufacturing;Farming;Handicrafts
- State: All-India

## Finance
- Loan: ₹1000000–₹10000000
- Subsidy: 0%
- Interest: As per lending bank
- Tenure: 7 years

## Documents
Aadhaar;PAN;Business Plan;Incubator Recommendation
