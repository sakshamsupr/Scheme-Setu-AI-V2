# Scheme Setu AI — researched evidence/case-study sources

These are real government/official sources used for the "Why this scheme?" evidence layer where an individual or programme-level example was found.

1. PMEGP — Ajai Gramodyog, Varanasi: KVIC documents a ₹5 lakh PMEGP bank loan, a handicrafts/stone-carving unit, later reporting ₹10 lakh annual turnover and employment for 10 people.
   https://kviconline.gov.in/pmegp/pmegpweb/docs/successstoryfile/20150623105116.pdf

2. NSSH — Ministry of MSME Annual Report 2023-24: 1,27,407 existing and aspiring SC/ST entrepreneurs benefited under NSSH components by 31 March 2024; SC/ST MSE procurement value reported at ₹1,406.57 crore in FY 2023-24.
   https://www.msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/FINALMSMEANNUALREPORT2023-24ENGLISH.pdf

3. PM SVANidhi — MoHUA programme evidence: 54.70 lakh loans worth ₹7,108.16 crore disbursed by 16 Aug 2023; 41% of beneficiaries were women vendors; 20.74 lakh loans repaid.
   https://mohua.gov.in/upload/whatsnew/64f1715fac04bmohua-ebook-single-pgs21.pdf

4. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme — Filmboard Movie Technologies: Startup India identifies it as a Seed Fund beneficiary with ₹15 lakh debt-linked support through an incubator, later reporting ₹4.79 crore external funding and more than 17 jobs.
   https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/bloglist/blogs/growth-potential.html

5. Mahila Coir Yojana — Coir Board reports and beneficiary records are published on the Board's official reports page.
   https://coirboard.gov.in/?page_id=187

6. SCLCSS / NSSH — the official MSME dashboard reports 2,841 beneficiaries and ₹321.88 crore of subsidy released across the reported fiscal years.
   https://sclcss.msme.gov.in/Common/Home.aspx

For schemes without a named individual case study in the current research set, the UI deliberately shows an "Official evidence layer" note rather than inventing a beneficiary story.
